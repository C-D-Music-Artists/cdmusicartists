Artist

ROC-Man is an independent rock artist, composer and multi-instrumentalist from Germany.

With more than three decades of experience as a musician, he has played bass, guitar, keyboards and vocals while continuously developing his own style of original rock music.

Today, ROC-Man writes, arranges, performs and produces every song himself. Every release is created from the first musical idea to the final production under the label C.D. Music Artists.

His music combines powerful rock elements with memorable melodies and authentic songwriting, creating a distinctive sound that reflects passion, experience and artistic independence.
